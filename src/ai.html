Why this path

Most AI products fail at the last mile — the product.

I already know how to turn complex systems into fast, secure, usable web and mobile apps. Understanding the models underneath lets me design AI features that actually work, not just wrap an API.

  • Understand models deeply — build them from scratch first
  • Learn by shipping — one real project per phase
  • Bring AI into fintech and mobile, where I already have depth
